# Usage guidance: VLKK and LKI

Read this page when choosing the spoken form of a number, date, time or
measurement, after the [rule router](published-rule-families/README.md) has
classified the span. It summarizes published guidance from the State
Commission of the Lithuanian Language (VLKK) and the Institute of the
Lithuanian Language (LKI). Examples in quotation marks are the sources' own;
the arrows show a written input and the spoken form the guidance implies.

The guidance selects among readings that context already allows. It does not
license a reading the sentence does not support, and it does not settle every
case: where it is silent (see the end of this page), use context or preserve
and report the span.

## Numeral forms

- **Collective numerals with pluralia tantum.** Before a noun used only in the
  plural (`metai`, `durys`, `rungtynės`, `varžybos`, `staklės`), 2–9 take the
  collective numeral, including the last word of a compound: "keturiomis (=
  ketveriomis) durimis", "Dvidešimt septynios (= Dvidešimt septynerios)
  staklės". So `4 metų laikotarpiui` → `ketverių metų laikotarpiui`, `2
  rungtynes` → `dvejas rungtynes`. Sources: [VLKK error list
  5.2](https://vlkk.lt/aktualiausios-temos/didziosios-klaidos/formu-vartojimo),
  [VLKK 2771](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2771-pagrindiniai-skaitvardziai),
  [VLKK 3183](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/3183-dauginiai-skaitvardziai),
  [LKI](https://administracinekalba.lki.lt/index.php?antr=d&str=dauginiai).
- **One with pluralia tantum.** Both the plural of `vienas` and `vieneri` are
  standard: "pasirinktinai vartojama vieni ir vieneri metai". So `1 metus` →
  `vienus metus` or `vienerius metus`, `1 rungtynes` → `vienas rungtynes` or
  `vienerias rungtynes`. Sources: [VLKK
  4124](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/4124-vieni-vieneri),
  [LKI](https://administracinekalba.lki.lt/index.php?antr=v&str=vieneri).
- **Nouns with both numbers take plain cardinals.** `4 dienas` → `keturias
  dienas`, never a collective. Source: [VLKK
  3183](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/3183-dauginiai-skaitvardziai).
- **An ending after a hyphen is read as written.** Digits take a hyphenated
  ending that keeps the soft-stem `i` ("1-as", "1-o", "1-am", "1-ą"). The
  2022 rules allow cardinal as well as ordinal endings: `6-ių darbuotojų` →
  `šešių darbuotojų`. With a plurale tantum a cardinal 2–9 would be
  collective, so `6-i metai` is the ordinal `šešti metai`, not `šešeri`.
  Sources: [VLKK spelling rules
  2022](https://vlkk.lt/media/public/Lietuviu_kalbos_rasyba.pdf) 11.1.8,
  [VLKK
  3566](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/3566-bruksnelis-skaiciai-skaitmenys-galunes).
- **A bare digit can be an ordinal.** Administrative and scientific texts
  write the numbers of structural parts without an ending: "2 skyrius", "15
  straipsnis", "3 priedas". Choose the reading that agrees with the noun:
  `6 kategorijoje` → `šeštoje kategorijoje`. Sources: spelling rules 11.1.8
  comment 2, [VLKK
  3566](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/3566-bruksnelis-skaiciai-skaitmenys-galunes).
- **A year is an ordinal.** "2003 metai (sk. du tūkstančiai tretieji metai)".
  Only the last word of the year is an ordinal. Sources: spelling rules 11.6.3,
  [VLKK topic: numbers](https://vlkk.lt/aktualiausios-temos/rasyba/skaiciai),
  [LKI](https://administracinekalba.lki.lt/index.php?antr=m&str=metai).
- **A day of the month is a feminine ordinal.** "lapkričio devinto (=
  devintą)", "nuo liepos penkiolikto (= penkioliktos)". So `lapkričio 9 d.` →
  `lapkričio devintą dieną` or `devintąją dieną`, never `devinto`. Sources:
  [VLKK error list
  5.1.2](https://vlkk.lt/aktualiausios-temos/didziosios-klaidos/formu-vartojimo),
  [LKI: diena](http://kanceliariniaipatarimai.lki.lt/Straipsnis.asp?nKodas=060047),
  [LKI: data](http://kanceliariniaipatarimai.lki.lt/Straipsnis.asp?nKodas=060019).
- **Compound cardinals decline every word except the tens.** "šešis šimtus
  keturiasdešimt aštuonis kilometrus", "tūkstančio šimto aštuoniasdešimt
  penkių vaikų". Source: [VLKK
  2769](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2769-samplaikiniai-kiekiniai-skaitvardziai).
- **Thousands, millions and billions decline like counted nouns.**
  `tūkstantis`, `milijonas` and `milijardas` take the form a noun takes after
  their count, and the count declines with the sentence, every word but the
  tens: "dvidešimt penki tūkstančiai žmonių", "tūkstančio šimto
  aštuoniasdešimt penkių vaikų". After a final 1 the scale word is singular
  and after 2–9 plural, both in the number's case; after 10–20, a round ten
  or a hundred it is the genitive plural. A count of one is not spoken:
  `1185` is `tūkstantis šimtas aštuoniasdešimt penki`, not `vienas
  tūkstantis`. So `25 000 žmonių` →
  `dvidešimt penki tūkstančiai žmonių`, `skyrė 2 000 000 eurų` → `skyrė du
  milijonus eurų`, `su 300 000 eurų` → `su trimis šimtais tūkstančių eurų`.
  Source: [VLKK
  2769](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2769-samplaikiniai-kiekiniai-skaitvardziai).
- **After a genitive-governing number the noun stays genitive plural.** After
  10–20, a round ten, a hundred or a scale word the counted noun is a genitive
  plural in every case, while the number itself takes the sentence's case:
  "perskaičiau dešimt puslapių", "dvidešimties metų jaunuolis", "pietūs
  trisdešimčiai žmonių". So `su 300 keleivių` → `su trimis šimtais keleivių`,
  not `su trys šimtai keleivių`. Sources: [VLKK
  2769](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2769-samplaikiniai-kiekiniai-skaitvardziai),
  [VLKK
  2770](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2770-sudurtiniai-skaitvardziai-linksniavimas).
- **Compound ordinals decline only the last word.** "penki šimtai
  aštuoniasdešimt penkto". Source: [VLKK
  14309](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/14309-samplaikiniai-kelintiniai-skaitvardziai).
- **Tens.** The declined forms (`dvidešimties`, `trisdešimčiai`) are
  standard, and in the nominative and accusative the standard language tends
  to use `dešimt` rather than `dešimtį`: "perskaičiau dešimt puslapių".
  The sources decline a ten that ends the number, in the genitive, dative and
  instrumental (`dvidešimties`, `dvidešimčiai`, `dvidešimčia`); the
  undeclined form stays standard there. Inside a compound, including before a
  scale word, a ten is not declined: `su 20 000 eurų` → `su dvidešimt
  tūkstančių eurų`.
  Sources: [VLKK
  2770](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2770-sudurtiniai-skaitvardziai-linksniavimas),
  [VLKK 12312](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/12312-keliasdesimt-linksniavimas).
- **The case after `apie`.** An approximate amount is nominative with an
  intransitive verb and accusative with a transitive one: "Renginyje dalyvavo
  apie dvidešimt penki tūkstančiai žmonių", "Nupirko apie tris kilogramus
  obuolių". Source: [VLKK
  2769](https://vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/2769-samplaikiniai-kiekiniai-skaitvardziai).
- **An amount after a measure verb is accusative.** "Jo ūgis siekia dviejų
  metrų (= du metrus)". Source: [VLKK error list
  3.2.1](https://vlkk.lt/aktualiausios-temos/didziosios-klaidos/linksniu-vartojimo).
- **A time span takes `per` with the accusative.** "per tris dienas", "laike
  10 dienų (= per dešimt dienų)". Sources: [VLKK error list
  3.6](https://vlkk.lt/aktualiausios-temos/didziosios-klaidos/linksniu-vartojimo),
  [LKI: laikas](http://kanceliariniaipatarimai.lki.lt/Straipsnis.asp?nKodas=180002).

## Written forms you will meet

The standard forms below come from the
[2022 spelling rules](https://vlkk.lt/media/public/Lietuviu_kalbos_rasyba.pdf)
unless noted. Input may also use forms the guidance corrects; classify them by
the same semantics, but never repair a value to make it fit.

| Class | Standard written forms | Forms the guidance corrects |
|---|---|---|
| Percentage | `5 procentai`, `5 proc.`, `5 %` (symbol after a space) | |
| Decimal | `1,5 kg` (decimal comma) | `1.5 kg` |
| Numeric date | `2018-01-15`, `2018 01 15` (year first, two-digit month and day) | `15.01.2018`, `25-04-2020` (day first) |
| Mixed date | `2018 m. rugpjūčio 6 d.`; `m.` and `d.` are read `metų` and `diena` | `2003 m. gegužės mėn. 7 d.`, `2020 m. balandžio 03 d.` |
| Clock time | `9.00 val.`, `9 val. 5 min.`, `10:10` (colon, no `val.`) | |
| Duration | `val.`, `min.`, `sek.` in general text; `h`, `min`, `s` in scientific text | |
| Range | `1918–1940 m.`, `15–20 proc.` (en dash, no spaces); `nuo … iki …` | `8-13 °C` (hyphen) |
| Money | `5 Eur`, `5 Eur 8 ct`, `EUR`, symbols after the number | `€ 12,50` (symbol first) |
| Units | `0,1 m`, `25 l`, `220 V` (after a space, no period) | |
| Large number | `3 000`, `15 000`, `1 000 000` (groups of three; a no-break space is recommended in running text; a dot only in data for computer processing) | `3,000` (no comma between groups) |
| Telephone | `8 222 22 222`, `(8 5) 333 3333` (spaces, no hyphens); the national prefix `0` replaces `8`: `(0 5) 272 4520` | `8-5-333 3333` |

A grouped large number is one value: read `1 000 000` as `milijonas`, never
as separate numbers. A comma is the decimal separator, so `3,000` is a
decimal, not three thousand; preserve and report it when context does not
settle which was meant. Digits grouped like a telephone number are a
telephone number only after a telephone cue (`tel.`, `telefonas`, `mob.`).

Sources for dates: [VLKK resolution on dates
(2001)](https://www.vlkk.lt/vlkk-nutarimai/nutarimai/del-datos-rasymo),
[VLKK 657](https://www.vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/657-data-datos-rasymas). For
times: [VLKK 1563](https://www.vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/1563-laikas-valandos). For
percentages: [VLKK 7369](https://www.vlkk.lt/konsultacijos/7369-procentai-tarpas).
For grouping: [VLKK topic:
numbers](https://vlkk.lt/aktualiausios-temos/rasyba/skaiciai). The `0` prefix
is VLKK's own contact number; the regulator RRT replaced `8` with `0` by
2025 ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2212150/lithuania-starts-replacing-8-with-0-as-phone-number-prefix)).

## Capitals in titles and names

Letter case is secondary for spoken output. A number word that is part of a
title or name may keep the capital the spelling rules give it: the first word
of historical names (8.8.1: `po 2-ojo pasaulinio karo` → `po Antrojo
pasaulinio karo`), official document titles (8.5.1) and organization names
(8.2.1.1). Otherwise write number words in lower case. Never abstain over
case alone.

## Where the guidance is silent

No VLKK or LKI entry found settles these; decide from context or preserve and
report the span:

- an hour with a day-period word (`9 val. vakaro`);
- Roman numerals as list markers (`IV.`, `IV)`);
- whether a bare digit before a noun in the dative is ordinal or cardinal
  (`9 tarnybai`: `devintai` or `devynių`);
- the case after `sverti` (by analogy with `siekti`, the accusative);
- how to read a telephone number aloud. The publication's rule K46 (see the
  [telephone branch](published-rule-families/03-telephones-codes-and-urls.md))
  reads it digit by digit, in the nominative whatever the sentence's case,
  with the plus sign as `plius`: `Telefonas 8 612 34567` → `Telefonas
  aštuoni šeši vienas du trys keturi penki šeši septyni`.
